Testing a 4-player KO in Postman
    - Create four players (unique firebaseUid each).
    - POST /api/tournament/ko with {"partyIds":[1,2,3,4],"partyType":"SINGLES","frameCount":3}.
    - GET /api/tournament/{id}/fixtures and note the matchIds.
    - For each match, GET /api/match/{matchId}/slots (the frames already exist),
        then POST /api/frame/{frameId}/result for every frame.
        A match only resolves once all its frames are recorded.
    - POST /api/tournament/{id}/ko/advance creates round 1.
        Repeat for round 1, and the advance after the final completes the tournament and sets the podium.

    For round robin,
        use "/roundrobin", play the matches, then GET .../standings and POST .../complete.
